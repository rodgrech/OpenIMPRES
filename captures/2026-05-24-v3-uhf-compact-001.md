## Early Comparison To VHF

VHF repeatedly shows:

```text
10:38:1B:*
```

UHF instead repeatedly shows:

```text
10:3C:6C:*
10:9C:14:*
10:75:*
10:1C:*
```

This supports the idea that some early `10:*` transaction families are
battery-specific and may include serial/unique identity fields. However, this
capture was taken while UHF was in conditioning mode, so some differences may be
mode/status fields rather than identity fields.
